ShiftBrief privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ShiftBrief is a Forge app for Jira Service Management and Confluence. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

What ShiftBrief reads

With the permissions of the person using it, ShiftBrief reads from Jira:

  • work items of the service project it is opened in: key, title, status, priority, created, updated and resolved dates, links, SLA cycles and the history of status, priority and assignee changes;
  • assignees and the people who hand over or acknowledge: account ID and display name;
  • field, status and priority definitions of the site, to read SLAs and priorities correctly;
  • from Confluence, only the space a user chooses for the shift log, and the shift log page in it.

What ShiftBrief stores, and where

Everything is stored in Forge storage, which is hosted by Atlassian, in your site's own installation:

WhatPurposeHow long
Handovers: the brief, notes, who handed over and whenThe handover screen and the work item trail30 days
Acknowledgements, takeovers and escalations (account ID and time)Who took ownership of what30 days
Desk settings: changeover times, time zone, duty lead, escalation delayConfigure each deskUntil changed or uninstalled
Field, status and priority definitionsFaster pages1 hour

What ShiftBrief writes

  • Jira: one comment on a critical work item that nobody acknowledged within the escalation delay. It states only what the handover recorded about that item and mentions the desk's duty lead.
  • Confluence: the weekly "Shift log" page, only when a user publishes it, in the space they choose, with their own permissions.

Sharing

ShiftBrief does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone. It uses no analytics, no trackers and no third-party services.

Removing data

Uninstalling ShiftBrief deletes its Forge storage for your site. Handovers expire after 30 days.
