Ripple privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Ripple is a Forge app for Jira and Confluence. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

What Ripple reads

With the permissions of the person using it, Ripple reads from Jira:

  • projects and work items: key, title, status, type, priority, due date, parent and links;
  • assignees: account ID, display name, avatar and whether the account is active;
  • resolved work from the last 365 days, used only as per-person counts per project.

What Ripple stores, and where

Everything is stored in Forge storage, which is hosted by Atlassian, in your site's own installation:

WhatPurposeHow long
Each user's organisation mapOpens the map quickly24 hours, rebuilt hourly
Planned absences (account ID and dates)The team calendarUntil 30 days after the absence ends
Daily risk scores per personThe week-on-week trend on the dashboard40 days
Report settings and the last report summaryThe weekly Confluence pageUntil the user stops the report or uninstalls
A marker per work item that already got an alertPrevents repeated comments60 days

Each user's map is stored under their own account and never shown to anyone else.

What Ripple writes

  • Jira: reassignments and comments, only after a user confirms them, and with that user's permissions. One app comment on a risky assignment, which states only what the work item itself shows. Jira admins can switch these comments off.
  • Confluence: a "Team risk map" page, only in the space a user chooses.

Sharing

Ripple does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone. It uses no analytics, no trackers and no third-party services.

Removing data

Uninstalling Ripple deletes its Forge storage for your site. Individual absences can be removed in the app at any time.
