Scope Trail privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Scope Trail is a Forge app for Jira and Confluence. It runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform. It has no servers of its own and sends no data outside Atlassian.

What Scope Trail reads

With the permissions of the person using it, Scope Trail reads from Jira:

  • work items of the sprint being explained and of its project: key, title, type, status, priority, estimate, sprint, parent, links, created and resolved dates, and the history of sprint, estimate and status changes;
  • the people who made those changes and the assignees: account ID and display name;
  • field and status definitions of the site, to find the sprint and estimate fields.

Names and dates of boards and sprints are read by the app itself, and only for projects the person can already see.

From Confluence, Scope Trail reads only the space a user chooses for a retro page, and that page.

What Scope Trail stores, and where

Scope Trail stores no work items, people or sprint data. It computes every trail when it is opened. The only stored data is a cache in Forge storage, hosted by Atlassian in your site's own installation:

WhatPurposeHow long
Field and status definitions of the siteFaster pages1 hour

What Scope Trail writes

  • Jira: nothing.
  • Confluence: a "Scope trail" retro page, only when a user publishes it, in the space they choose, with their own permissions.

Sharing

Scope Trail does not sell, share or transfer data to anyone. It uses no analytics, no trackers, no AI models of its own and no third-party services.

Removing data

Uninstalling Scope Trail deletes its Forge storage for your site. Retro pages stay in Confluence until you delete them.
